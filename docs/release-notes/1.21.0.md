This release separates the people a ZEV legally consists of from the logins
that use OpenZEV. A ZEV no longer has one owner account: it has dated roles,
and access is granted per community, so one login can manage one ZEV, look
at another and rent in a third. If you import SDAT files, or CSV files with
timezone offsets, read **Tariff bands follow Swiss time** before you
regenerate draft invoices.

### Parties, dated roles and access per community

Every person or organisation of a ZEV is now a *party*, with or without a
login. A participant can be a company or a household: the participant form
has a person/organisation choice and a second name line, and **Same person
or organisation as** adds a second participation for someone already in the
ZEV. Issuer, representative toward the grid operator and landowner are dated
roles. Each document names the issuer of its own date: an invoice the one on
its period's last day, a contract its contract date, an annual statement
31 December.

All of it is on one tab, **Settings → People & access**:

![The People & access tab: issuer, representative and landowner roles with their logins, and other people with their access levels](screenshots/1.21.0-people-access.png)

The issuer and the representative manage the ZEV through their login for as
long as they hold the role. Anyone else gets access to the whole ZEV only
when you give it, as **Manager** or **Read only**, on their row or with
**Give access**; people without an account are invited by email. A read-only viewer, such as a
bookkeeper, sees and downloads everything a manager does but changes nothing.
Managers do this themselves, without an admin, and OpenZEV refuses any change
that would leave a ZEV without a manager.

Participant logins work as before: a participant linked to an account sees
their own consumption, invoices and annual statements, with no role or grant
needed. What is new is that one login covers every relationship: the
community switcher lists each ZEV with the account's relation to it. A tenant
who moves out keeps the invoices sent to them, and nothing else.

Existing ZEVs migrate as they were: the owner's party becomes issuer and
landowner, and the owner keeps managing. An owner who was not a participant
leaves the ZEV without an issuer, as before; **Overview** now warns that its
invoices carry no QR bill. Buildings, with an address per metering point,
are not part of this release.

### Tariff bands follow Swiss time

Readings are stored in UTC, and billing also asked its calendar questions in
UTC. For SDAT and offset-carrying CSV data, HT/NT and weekday bands were off
by one hour in winter and two in summer (06:30 in summer billed as low rate,
Saturday 00:30 counted as Friday), and periods and tariff validity started at
UTC midnight. Offset-less CSV happened to bill correctly.

Every calendar question is now answered in Europe/Zurich, and charts and PDFs
show Swiss hours. The import wizard asks how to read timestamps without an
offset. Approved and sent invoices are unchanged; regenerate your drafts.

### Annual ZEV report

**Reports** shows an annual report per ZEV: self-consumption and
self-sufficiency against the previous year, month by month, and each
participant's consumption, ZEV share and savings against the grid rate. The
figures are the dashboard's and the annual statements', so they agree. The
annual-statement ZIP moved here, under **Annual documents**.

### Also new

- **Generation behind the meter (surplus metering)** on a metering point: its
  holder shows no self-sufficiency rate instead of a misleading 1–2 %.
- Two-factor authentication is one switch, **Require two-factor
  authentication for every account**, under **Platform → System Settings →
  Security**.

### Fixes worth knowing about

- **Participants could see invoices before they were sent.** Draft and
  approved invoices appeared under *My invoices* and counted toward their
  annual statement. Now only sent invoices do.
- **Re-rendering a sent invoice used today's data.** Issuer, QR-bill IBAN,
  VAT number and recipient address were read live, so a regenerated invoice
  could differ from the one delivered. Invoices now keep a copy, frozen on
  approval. Existing invoices were filled from today's data; check any sent
  invoice whose IBAN or address has changed since.

### Under the hood

Access is decided per ZEV in one module, and a write without manager rights
fails before any view code runs. ADR 0026 records the Swiss-time decision,
ADRs 0027 and 0028 the access and party model.

### Upgrading

Fourteen migrations, most rewriting data: ten in `zev` (access grants and
issuer/landowner roles from each owner, one party per participant taking over
its name and address, `Zev.owner` dropped, the behind-the-meter flag), two in
`accounts` (the 2FA switch, platform roles collapsed to `admin`/`user`), one
in `invoices` (the issuer/recipient copy) and one in `metering` (the import's
timestamp zone).

**Two-factor authentication:** a policy that covered any role now covers
every account, and the grace period restarts at the upgrade.

**Offset-less CSV imports** need re-anchoring before you regenerate drafts.
Nothing moves automatically, and invoiced batches are refused without
`--allow-invoiced`:

```
python manage.py reanchor_readings --list
python manage.py reanchor_readings --batch <id> [--apply]
```

**Breaking changes:**

- `User.role` is `admin` or `user`; per-ZEV access is in `memberships`.
  `/auth/me` drops `zev_name` and `zev_count`. A non-admin without access
  gets 403 from management endpoints instead of empty lists.
- ZEVs have no `owner`; the API returns a read-only `issuer`. An admin
  creating a ZEV through the API grants nobody access, and a ZEV restored
  from a backup has no managers until an admin adds one.
- The dashboard summary's `role` is now `summary_kind`;
  `mfa_required_roles` is now `mfa_required`.
- Templates: `owner_participant.*` and `zev.owner.*` alias `issuer.*`, but
  `zev.owner.username` renders empty.
- Transfer archives are format 5, which 1.20 cannot import.
