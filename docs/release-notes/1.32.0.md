This release lets you act on a late meter-data correction from the invoice itself: see what it would change, then replace the invoice in one step. Most record tables are now sortable, and the ZEV settings and tariff screens got a consistency pass. If your community sends invoice emails with the platform default text in German, French or Italian, read the first fix.

### Late corrections: preview and replace

Since 1.27.0 a late correction flags the invoice with **Data review** and leaves it alone. This release adds the next step. The invoice detail page now shows the expected total before you change anything: current amount, expected amount, the difference in CHF, and any energy that moves between local and grid. The preview writes nothing and uses no invoice number; confirming calculates again.

What you can do depends on the status:

| Invoice | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Draft | **Regenerate invoice** |
| Approved or sent, unpaid | **Create replacement invoice**: cancels the original and calculates a new draft in one confirmed step. If the calculation fails, the original stays valid. |
| Paid | **Correct invoice → Reverse the invoice** first, then **Create replacement invoice** |

The cancelled original keeps its amount and PDF, and its PDF is labelled **Issued invoice – original**. A flagged draft's stored PDF says **Stored PDF – changes not yet included**. The replacement opens as a draft; nothing is approved or sent for you. The detail page now also offers the next ordinary step, **Approve** and then **Send Email**, so you no longer go back to **Billing** for it.

To see what changed, **Review corrections** opens the invoice's evidence, and an import's **Protocol** has a new **Reading changes** list with old and new kWh per meter and interval, 50 per page. An import keeps at most 10,000 changes and warns when it had to drop some. SDAT findings in the protocol now appear in your language with the invalid value and the XML location.

Scripts can use two new endpoints on an invoice, `POST …/calculation-preview/` and `POST …/replace/`. Cancelling and generating separately still works.

Limits: paid invoices are never replaced directly. The correction handling is tested against the official SDAT-CH sample and demo data, not yet against a grid operator's live correction delivery.

### Sortable tables

Click a column heading to sort participants (dashboard and **Billing**), **Documents**, one-off charges, tariffs within a category, metering points and most admin tables. Click again to reverse. Amounts and dates sort by value, names in your language's order.

A table sorts only with two or more rows. The audit log and held readings stay chronological, since they are paged on the server. Calculation tables such as invoice line items and tariff composition keep their fixed order. On narrow screens the participant and billing lists become cards without sort controls; metering points keep theirs.

### Also new

- **Audit log in your language.** Events are named in German, English, French or Italian instead of one English sentence; the stored sentence is under **Technical details**. At phone width rows become cards.
- **ZEV settings.** A dot marks tabs with unsaved edits and the save bar names them. The tab strip scrolls instead of wrapping, viewers see flat readable fields, and **People & access** is a table with a **…** menu per person.
- **Billing overview.** Items that need review look different from problems, and finished checks sit in their own **Checked and complete** section.
- **Tariffs** now has **Tariff components** and **Price progression** as tabs. When a version leaves hours uncovered, the warning offers **Add a band for these hours**, prefilled so you only set the price. A dynamic source's URL and coverage show without opening a disclosure, and a failed price fetch is explained in your language.
- **Charts** on screen and in the PDFs use the green and gold palette: gold for energy from the community, slate blue for the grid.
- **Wording.** German terms are consistent (*Tarifperiode*, *Freigabe*, *Zuweisung*), counts of one read in the singular, and dates use the app's language rather than the browser's.
- **Row actions** use icon-only Edit and Delete buttons with tooltips; a lone overflow action shows directly.
- **Smaller things.** Switching community from **Tariffs** now asks before dropping an unsaved tariff form. The CSV overwrite confirmation reports matching readings instead of overstating how many will be overwritten. Tokens, keys and recovery codes shown once now share one layout with **Copy** beside the value. The impersonation notice spans the whole window, and the template editors work on narrow screens.

### Fixes worth knowing about

**Invoice emails in English for non-English communities.** If a community's invoice email text is set to **Use platform default** (**Documents & emails**), the email read "Invoice … / Dear …" in English whatever the invoice language. The shipped default existed only in English. It now exists in all four languages and follows the community's invoice language. A custom text, per community or platform-wide, is unchanged and still one text for all languages. To check what went out, open **Billing → Emails** and **View history**.

**Export / transfer text.** The card said a deactivated community could be reactivated at any time; only an administrator can. The text now says so.

### Under the hood

Tables share one sorting implementation, and the frontend moved to new major versions of react-table, i18next, vitest and stylelint. The backend and published images now build on Python 3.15.

### Upgrading

One migration, additive: `tariffs.0021_dynamic_fetch_diagnostics` adds a field for translatable fetch errors; no downtime is expected. No new settings and nothing removed. Besides the two invoice endpoints above, the invoice email template endpoint accepts an optional `?language=`, and dynamic sources, VSE imports and ZEV archive imports gain nullable `*_diagnostic(s)` fields beside their unchanged English messages. If you run the backend outside the published image, it needs Python 3.15.
