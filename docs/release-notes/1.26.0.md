This release lets you bill a one-off cost, such as postage for the invoice run or a repair, to your participants without turning it into a tariff. It is the only feature in 1.26.0, and it has one rule to learn: a period's charges freeze when its first invoice is approved.

### One-off charges

A cost that happens once does not belong in the tariff list, on the participation contract or in the tariff overview. Record it as a **charge** under **Billing → Invoices**: pick the period, then use **Add charge** in the **Charges** section below the invoice table. Give it a description, the total amount, a date, and who pays, either all participants or a selection. When more than one person pays, the split is equal or by allocation weight. A **Who pays what** table in the dialog shows every share before you save.

![The Charges section under a period's invoices, with the note that an approved invoice freezes it](screenshots/1.26.0-charges.png)

Each participant who pays gets the charge as a line under **Other charges**, last on the invoice and PDF. Because the charge lives outside the invoice, the line comes back when you regenerate a draft.

Three rules decide the result:

- **The date decides.** The invoice whose period contains the date bills the charge, and the people who pay are the participants on that date. Someone who joined after it, or left before it, pays nothing. A cost you find late gets a date in the next period.
- **Shares are rounded one by one**, as for shared fees. CHF 10.00 over three people bills CHF 3.33 each, CHF 9.99 in all. The dialog says so when rounding leaves an amount unrecovered.
- **VAT is your choice.** **Taxable** (the default) puts the charge in the VAT base. **Pass-through** is for an expense you pay in the participant's name and pass on at cost: no VAT is added, and the PDF lists it as *Expenses passed on (no VAT)*. Check with your tax adviser that a cost qualifies before you use it. In a community that is not VAT-registered, or that folds VAT into its prices, the charge is billed as entered.

**The period freezes.** Once any invoice of the period is approved, sent or paid, you can no longer add, edit or delete its charges, even those that invoice does not carry. This keeps every invoice of the period on one set of charges. Add all charges before you approve the first invoice, then regenerate the drafts. Afterwards, correct the invoice or record the cost in a later period.

**Drafts do not update themselves.** A draft generated before you added, changed or deleted a charge keeps its old lines. The period's checklist shows a **Charges** step that counts those drafts, and it also warns about a charge that nobody pays, for example because no one was a participant on its date.

Charges cannot be negative, so they cannot credit a participant. They cannot recur, so a monthly fee stays a tariff. They cannot be computed per document. Managers and owners edit them in the app, viewers can only read them through the API, and participants see only the invoice line.

### Upgrading

Four migrations, all additive or relaxing, no data rewritten and no downtime expected, no configuration changes:

- `invoices.0026_variable_charges` creates the charge table and adds the **Other** invoice category
- `invoices.0027_invoice_pass_through` adds the pass-through total to invoices
- `invoices.0028_invoice_item_unit_price_width` widens the unit price on invoice lines
- `tariffs.0020_variable_charges` updates the category choices

Charges are part of the whole-ZEV export archive and of backups. Nothing is removed or changed for API clients, except that `/api/v1/invoices/charges/` is new and invoice items can carry the category `other`.
